Irish Mines

This listing is based on the published Mineral Statistics and therefore only records 19th and early 20th century workings. The fact that no production is recorded for some sites and for some periods does not necessarily mean that no ore was raised. Added to the list however are other sites where mining activity is known to have taken place. Sometimes the borderline between testing and working a mineral showing is not clear; hence sites which have no record of ore production are merely noted.
Alternative or erroneous spellings are listed alphabetically with bracketed reference (see - ) to standard Ordnance Survey spelling which is used throughout. Where the variant spelling is very close to the correct version it is merely noted in brackets. Sub-mines are gathered together under the main mining area in which they occur. Thus Cloan and Mountain are part of Allihies; Foxrock and Glendalough come under Luganure; Ballynoe and Shallee form part of Silvermines.
 Whilst MHTI endeavoured to ensure the copyright of all images and material presented resides with MHTI, as given by members on transfer to the archive, or used with external permission, it is possible that errors may have occurred. Contact naturalhistory@museum.ie if you believe your own copyright is contradicted or wish to have credit added to an image.
